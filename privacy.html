Privacy notice

How ERGPath uses information, the choices you have, and how to contact us.

Effective 30 October 2026

Who is responsible

ERGPath is operated by Pauric Briody in Ireland. Pauric handles support and privacy requests at support@ergpath.com. This notice covers the Android app, account and backup services, and ergpath.com. We do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

Free testing list

If you choose to join the free testing list, we collect your email address and record when you agreed to receive testing invitations and declared that you are aged 18 or over. We use this separate list to invite you to ERGPath’s free Android testing programme, based on your consent. Joining does not create an app account or subscribe you to general marketing.

The list is stored with Cloudflare. Cloudflare Turnstile checks submissions for abuse and processes technical browser and network information for that purpose. We also keep short-lived, keyed network identifiers and request counts to limit repeated submissions; raw IP addresses are not stored in the tester-list database. These controls are separate from optional website analytics.

To withdraw and leave the list, email support@ergpath.com from the address you registered. We retain your list entry until you withdraw or the testing recruitment list closes. Confirmation is displayed on the website; this form does not send an automatic confirmation email. Invitations are sent separately when testing is ready.

Information we use

We receive sign-in identifiers and relevant email/profile information through Firebase Authentication. We use these to authenticate you and keep accounts separate. The app stores profile settings, weight, FTP, custom workouts, plans, calendar entries, rides and training history. Compatible connected equipment can supply heart rate, power, cadence, speed, distance and timing information. These support workouts, training metrics and progress. Estimates are not medical diagnoses.

Supported information can be backed up to Cloudflare for restoration. A local save or successful sign-in does not prove a backup completed. Backups depend on connectivity and successful processing. If you contact support, we receive your message, email address and attachments. Please do not send passwords, sign-in codes or unnecessary sensitive information.

Health information and your choice

Heart rate, body weight, ride/sensor history and FTP records may reveal information about health. ERGPath's health-data consent control records an explicit, separate choice for training features, progress and cloud backups. An age declaration or sign-in is not that consent.

In app versions with this control, use Manage account → Health-data consent to withdraw. Declining leaves general workout descriptions, support and account management available. Withdrawal stops affected features and starts removal of affected history, sensor records, FTP tests, profile FTP/weight and related backup content. It keeps your sign-in account, username, custom workouts, calendar and connection credentials. Cleanup can take time and failures may need support; pending means removal is not yet confirmed.

The updated control is being introduced in the next app release. If your installed version does not show it, contact support@ergpath.com to arrange your request. An email is not an automatic deletion command: we verify account control and confirm the outcome. An offline device receives a withdrawal made elsewhere when it reconnects. Files already exported and activities already sent to Strava are outside this cleanup. Withdrawal is separate from deleting your entire account and does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Why we use information

  • We use necessary account and ordinary service information to provide the account, training and backup features you request (contract, GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • For health-data processing covered by the explicit control, we rely on consent (Article 6(1)(a)) and explicit consent for health information (Article 9(2)(a)).
  • We use limited security, abuse-prevention and deletion-verification records for our legitimate interests in protecting the service and preventing erased data being recreated (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • We handle privacy requests and required records to meet applicable legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)). Ordinary support helps provide the requested service.
  • Optional website analytics uses consent where required. The regional behaviour is described below; you can reject measurement through Analytics settings.

Sign-in information is needed for account services; training measurements are needed for features that use them. Strava connection, file import/export and website analytics are separate choices. We do not use your data for advertising profiles.

Age and training access

ERGPath is intended for adults aged 18 and over. When asked, you make an explicit declaration. We store the answer, declaration version and recording time, rather than asking for full date of birth or identity documents through this screen. Google sign-in does not verify age. An under-18 declaration restricts training access, while support and deletion remain available. Contact support about an incorrect declaration; correction is not automatic.

Training estimates are calculated from your settings and available measurements. Age restrictions use your submitted answer, not an inferred age. Confirmed devices can work offline and may not immediately receive a later restriction.

Providers and international processing

Google/Firebase provides authentication; Cloudflare hosts the website/backend and backups; Microsoft 365 through GoDaddy provides support email. These providers process the information needed to deliver and secure their services. If you connect Strava, credentials and upload records support the rides you choose to send, including optional automatic uploads. Strava handles received activities under its own privacy policy. Disconnecting or deleting ERGPath does not erase activities already on Strava.

Providers can process information outside Ireland and the EEA, including in the United States. The backup bucket uses Cloudflare's EU jurisdiction; this does not mean all authentication, delivery or technical processing remains in the EU. Provider terms describe applicable international-transfer safeguards, including standard contractual clauses and adequacy arrangements where applicable. Contact support for information about the safeguards relevant to your request.

Provider information: Google Cloud, Cloudflare, Microsoft, GoDaddy and Strava.

Device features

Bluetooth connects compatible equipment and can remember its name/address for reconnection. On Android 11 and earlier, Bluetooth discovery requires Android's location permission; ERGPath does not read GPS coordinates to record your location. Android's file picker handles selected imports and exports. External storage providers handle exported copies under their own terms.

Entertainment mode opens your chosen apps or websites and can show workout controls over them. The launch does not send your workout data to those services. They have their own terms. The app does not include an advertising SDK, third-party usage-analytics SDK or automatic crash-reporting service. Authentication and hosting still process technical information. Older app diagnostic logs may contain workout or file details; check logs before sharing them.

Website analytics and storage

Cloudflare delivers and protects the site and processes technical request information. Cloudflare Web Analytics helps us understand visits and performance. For visitors identified as in the EU, analytics starts after Accept. Outside the EU, or if location is unknown, it starts without a banner unless a saved Reject choice applies. You can reject or change your choice using Analytics settings on the homepage. Your browser remembers the choice for 180 days. Location estimates can be inaccurate. Reports filter known bots but estimate visits, not unique people.

Cloudflare describes seven days of detailed beacon data, followed by sampled or aggregated data, with reports available for six months. The privacy and deletion pages do not include our optional analytics script. The deletion page keeps an opaque progress receipt in that tab's session storage; it does not store your password or Firebase sign-in token in that receipt. Closing the tab loses that browser recovery route but does not stop accepted cleanup. Google sign-in and provider security functions can use their own browser storage.

Retention

We retain the account and saved history until you request deletion, including during breaks from training, unless affected information is removed following health-consent withdrawal. We do not automatically delete accounts for inactivity. Signing out or uninstalling does not delete the cloud account or successful backups.

Normal backups retain the latest completed version and up to two earlier versions. Older or incomplete objects are cleaned asynchronously. Minimal consent and deletion records are retained as needed to enforce your choices, handle requests and prevent stale clients restoring removed data. Deletion markers currently have no automatic expiry; their continued necessity is reviewed rather than expiring protection while old clients can still return.

Support cases are reviewed for removal 12 calendar months after resolution. Unresolved cases, disputes and justified legal holds are kept for their purpose and reviewed separately. This is not a promise that every provider recovery copy disappears on that date.

Provider security, authentication and recovery records have separate retention. At the time of this notice, the configured routine Worker log retention is three days; inspected Google Cloud ordinary/audit log periods are 30/400 days. Firebase describes authentication IP retention as a few weeks and removal of other authentication information from live/backup systems within 180 days after deletion starts. These are provider records, not continued access to a deleted ERGPath workout account. Periods can change with service configuration.

Delete your account

Use Delete Account in the app or delete your ERGPath account on this website. Sign in to the intended account and explicitly confirm deletion. If you cannot sign in, contact support for ownership verification. An email alone does not erase an account.

Deletion removes the sign-in account, scoped cloud backups, age/consent records and account-linked integration records. Temporary cleanup failures retry. Enrolled devices clean their local data after confirming server deletion; offline devices must reconnect. External exports and Strava activities remain. Minimal deletion proofs remain to prevent recreation. Older integration records whose ownership cannot safely be established require separate review; we do not delete another person's records by guessing.

Your rights and contact

Contact support@ergpath.com to request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection or portable data where applicable, or to withdraw consent. We may need proportionate information to verify control of the account. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes. For applicable GDPR requests we respond without undue delay, normally within one month; if a permitted extension is needed, we explain it within that first month.

You can raise concerns with the Irish Data Protection Commission or your competent supervisory authority. We use authenticated access, account separation and transport protection, but no service guarantees absolute security. This notice does not claim end-to-end encryption or EU-only processing.

Changes

We will update this notice and its date when practices change. Where a new notice or fresh choice is required, we provide it before relying on the change. Any future subscription will have separate payment information before charges are introduced.
