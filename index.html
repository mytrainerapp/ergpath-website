Before you clip in

A few useful details.

What equipment do I need?

An Android phone or tablet, your bike and a compatible Bluetooth FTMS smart trainer. Available readings depend on your connected equipment. Trainer support varies by model and firmware.

Can I change how often I train?

Most plans offer several riding frequencies. Each plan card above shows its supported days per week, so you can choose an option that fits your schedule.

How does Entertainment Mode work?

ERGPath displays floating workout metrics and controls over other apps after you grant Android’s display-over-other-apps permission. You use your own entertainment apps and subscriptions.

What does the Strava connection do?

You can upload saved ERGPath rides to Strava, including optional automatic uploads. Importing your existing Strava activity history into ERGPath is not currently offered.

When can I download ERGPath?

ERGPath is preparing for release on Android. Download information will appear here when the app is publicly available.
