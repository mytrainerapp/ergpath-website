Review your ride totals.

The Overview tab brings completed rides, active ride time, distance, training stress, average heart rate and calories together. Choose a time range and switch the activity chart between duration and distance.

In this example: four rides, 3 hours 30 minutes and 105 km across the last four weeks.

Keep every ride in context.

Your riding journey.

Browse saved sessions, then open a ride to review its details. History shows the workout name, date, duration, distance and average power, with a summary of the selected period.

The four sessions shown here add up to the same totals as Overview. The Ramp Test is also recorded in the FTP example.

Follow your FTP over time.

A record of your test results.

The FTP tab shows your current setting, power relative to body weight when available, and accepted FTP test results. Review the trend and the change between tests.

This illustrative rider has a current FTP of 230 W at 75 kg (3.07 W/kg), up 10 W from their previous test. Example results are not a prediction of training gains.

We use optional analytics to understand visits and improve ERGPath.

More details

Cloudflare Web Analytics receives page and browser performance measurements. For visitors identified as being in the EU, analytics starts only after Accept. For visitors outside the EU, or whose location cannot be determined, analytics starts automatically without a banner unless a saved Reject choice applies. Cloudflare estimates the region from the connection; we do not ask for device location permission. You can reject analytics or change your choice using Analytics settings in the footer. Reports help us understand traffic and filter known bots; they count visits, not unique people. We remember your choice on this browser for 180 days. Change it anytime using Analytics settings in the footer. Rejecting analytics does not stop the technical processing needed to deliver and protect the site. For privacy questions, email support@ergpath.com.

Cloudflare describes seven days of detailed beacon data, followed by sampled or aggregated data, with reports accessible for six months. This is separate from your ERGPath app and workout data.
